# The Hotel Operator — Public-Safe Business Context

Website: https://thehoteloperator.com/

## Current internal phase

HOTEL AI DISCOVERY is in **Discovery**, not final product definition.

The public website is a positioning/acquisition surface. It must not be treated as proof that any service, pain point or implementation claim has been validated across the market.

## Focus

Primary segment:

- independent hotels;
- boutique hotels;
- small-to-medium 3–4 star properties.

Adjacent hospitality evidence is retained when relevant.

## Discovery architecture

Community Intelligence + Hotel Intelligence + Competitor Intelligence
→ Market Research + Lead Engine
→ Discovery Command Center
→ Research and/or human-approved Outreach
→ Discovery Call
→ New Market Evidence.

## Evidence posture

Distinguish verified fact, explicit public claim, first-party observation, inference and hypothesis.

The founder/operator has direct hotel-operations exposure, but observations from one property are not generalized to the whole market without independent evidence.

## Public/private boundary

Safe to keep in GitHub:

- The Hotel Operator brand;
- https://thehoteloperator.com/;
- target-segment description;
- high-level Discovery architecture;
- technical schemas/rules that contain no private data.

Do not commit:

- private Google Drive IDs unless explicitly approved for publication;
- raw research datasets;
- community member data;
- lead/contact lists;
- confidential hotel information;
- guest PII;
- credentials/tokens;
- private employment details.

Google Drive remains canonical for business knowledge and evidence. GitHub is canonical for code and technical version history.
